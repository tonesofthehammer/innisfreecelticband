ABOUT

Innisfree is a Canadian Celtic band bringing the spirit of Ireland to
audiences across Southern Ontario. Performing beloved Irish and Celtic
classics alongside unexpected hits reimagined in their distinctive
Celtic style, Innisfree delivers energetic, crowd-friendly performances.

Catch them every Thursday at Corktown Pub in Hamilton, or at
events throughout the Ontario region.
